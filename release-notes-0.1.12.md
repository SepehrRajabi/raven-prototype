# Raven 0.1.12

## Changed

- **A redesigned sidebar.** The sidebar now answers one question at a time. A switch at the top chooses between **Spaces** and **People** (your friends and groups). If you call them trees in Settings › Appearance, the switch says **Trees**. Raven remembers your choice on each device. It also switches for you when you open something from the other side, such as a conversation from a notification.
- **Live calls at the top.** When a call is in progress, a Live section above both lists shows a card for each live room. Each card shows the room, its space and the people inside, with their pictures and names. The room you are in comes first and shows who is speaking, followed by rooms with your friends in them. Clicking a card shows the room before you join, and clicking a person opens their profile. You can fold the section to a single line that still counts the live rooms; Raven remembers this on each device. When nothing is live, the section is not shown.
- **Find someone.** The People list has a search field. It finds your friends and groups, people you share a space with, and archived conversations. The last result searches the whole server for the same text.
- **One list of friends and groups.** Offline friends stay in the list, dimmed and at the end, instead of in a separate section. Groups keep their square picture and now show how many people they have.

## Removed

- **The "All conversations" page.** Everything it listed is in the People list or can be found with Find someone, including archived conversations.
- **The list of people from your spaces and the separate offline list** in the sidebar. People you share a space with appear when you search for them.
