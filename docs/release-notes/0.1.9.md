# Raven 0.1.9

## New

- **Mentions.** Type @ in a message to mention someone, or, in a text nest, another nest of the same tree. A mention of you reaches you even in a muted chat (not during Do not disturb), and the chat's row shows a small face until you read it.
- **Saved messages.** Keep messages for yourself: "Add to Saved messages" in a message's menu, or open Saved messages from the top of Friends and groups. A saved message shows who wrote it and opens the chat it came from.
- **Forwarding.** Select messages in a chat and forward them to another chat, with a note if you like. You can hide who wrote them or where they came from.
- **Swipe to reply.** Swipe left on a message to reply to it.
- **Names per space.** You can use a different name in each space. Moderators and the owner can set it for people below them. Direct messages, groups and profiles keep your usual name.
- **Profile pages from chat.** Click a name or picture in a chat to open that person's page. You have your own page too, under "Your profile" in the dock menu.
- **Seen in groups.** "Seen" now shows under the last message of a group or nest, whoever sent it.

## Changed

- Every Join now opens the nest's doorway first, so you see who is inside and what you will share before you go in.
- Profile pages, nest doorways and tree previews share one cleaner layout. A friend request on a profile is its own prompt.
- On a tree's page, a live voice nest shows once, as a Live now card, and text and voice nests use the same icons everywhere.
