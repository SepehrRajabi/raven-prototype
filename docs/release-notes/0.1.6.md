# Raven 0.1.6

## New

- Text chat grew up. You can send pictures: Raven shrinks them before they go, and you can add a caption. You can react to a message with up to three emoji, and see who reacted. There is an emoji picker, `:shortcodes:` suggest emoji as you type, and a message with only emoji shows them large.
- New messages can play a sound and show a notification. Direct messages and groups notify; text rooms stay quiet unless you turn them on. Mute any conversation from its menu. Both are in Settings › Messages.
- You can see who has seen the last message. Seeing works both ways: you only see it for people who share it with you. Four switches in Settings › Messages choose where you share it: direct messages, groups, private spaces and public spaces.
- Auto-level in the Mixer evens out how loud people's microphones are, so you stop reaching for the faders. Move someone's fader yourself and Auto-level leaves them alone until you press Resume. There is also a master volume for everything you hear.
- The voice overlay is now one card. You are a row in it like everyone else, with one speaking ring. It can show knocks and messages from text rooms, lets you mute, deafen or step away with a click, and shows weak connections. Almost everything about it has a switch in Settings › Voice overlay.
- "Back in a bit" and deafened now show for everyone in the call, on the stage, in the room and in the overlay.
- Persian text now uses a new font, IRANYekan.
- Joining a room that is stuck on "connecting" now has a Cancel button.

## Fixed

- If the page crashes in the middle of a call, Raven now recovers and puts you back in the call instead of leaving you with a blank window.
