# Raven 0.1.5

## New

- Background noise is now handled much better. Settings › Audio and video has four levels of noise suppression: Off, Standard, Strong (the new default) and Studio, the strongest. If Studio is too heavy for your computer, Raven quietly uses Strong for that call and tells you.
- Raven can play short, quiet sounds when you mute or unmute, when someone knocks on you, when a screen share starts or stops, when someone is about to join, and when people join or leave your room. Only you hear them. Turn each one on or off, or change their volume, in Settings › Audio and video › Sounds.
- You can now see who is watching a screen share: their pictures show on the share, and an eye shows beside their name in the room.
- Network activity can now show your connection to the server, like "42 ms · Good". Turn on "Connection" in its settings.
- When someone is joining but their connection is slow, the "joining" notice now says they're connecting, and they show as connecting in the room until they can hear and be heard.
- Rooms and text rooms can be renamed and deleted from their "…" menu, by the people allowed to manage them.
- Names in the voice overlay now sit on a soft background so they stay readable over any game. Choose Off, Light or Strong in Settings › Voice overlay.
- "Around" has its own colour now, a soft blue.
- A changed profile picture now shows everywhere at once, including the overlay.
- When you share a window and minimize it, the share pauses and shows its last picture, and everyone watching is told why.

## Fixed

- Stream info opens again from a share's menu.
- Saving hotkeys now says it saved.
- The device buttons' order moved to Settings › Appearance.
- Custom share and camera quality starts from the preset you were on and keeps the picture's shape.
- People joining a room no longer show up in the room and the overlay at different moments.
