# Raven 0.1.10

## Changed

- **Home lists your places.** Home now shows your trees, with what is live, waiting or unread first, so it is never empty once you belong somewhere.
- **Tree pages show what is alive.** A tree's page leads with what is happening now, in the order its owners set.
- **Nest doorways are clearer.** A nest's doorway says what joining does, all in one place.
- **A person's page offers one next step.** What it offers depends on how you know them.
- **Names per space go further.** Your name in a space now also shows in chat previews, member lists, system messages, typing indicators and calls in that space. Two people in a space can't use the same name.

## Fixed

- Port sharing in the Workshop stops when you sign out, switch accounts or turn it off, and a replaced link no longer cuts off the new one.
- Port sharing holds up better under bursts of traffic, and a failed UDP connection now shows as refused.
