# Raven 0.1.2

## New

- Raven remembers how loud you set each person in the Mixer, for their voice and their shared sound separately. It stays that way in later calls and after restarting Raven. Set someone back to 100% to forget it.
- If you start talking while you're muted, a short note above the microphone says so, with an Unmute button. It shows once each time you mute, then leaves you alone.
- When Raven switches your microphone or speaker on its own, for example because you plugged in a headset or your headphones ran out of battery, it now tells you which one it is using and why.
- When your connection makes you hard to hear, the bottom bar says "Sounding choppy" and explains what usually helps. The network readout also says how you sound to others.
