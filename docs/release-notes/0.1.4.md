# Raven 0.1.4

## New

- Friends and groups now share one list in the sidebar, so you no longer see the same person twice. A friend's row opens your conversation with them; a group's row opens the group. A tree lists only the nests that are live, have unread messages, or are open.
- Screen shares are now yours to choose. When someone starts sharing, Raven offers Watch instead of switching to it, and you can watch several shares at once. Settings has "Watch new shares automatically" if you prefer the old way.
- Screen shares look much smoother: they now arrive at the frame rate they are sent instead of a fraction of it.
- When someone shares their computer's sound or an app's sound, it shows as a small bubble beside them, with its own volume and mute.
- People joining and leaving a room now ease in and out instead of jumping.
- You can drag the sidebar wider or narrower.
- You can rearrange the buttons at the bottom of a call: right-click one, or choose "Arrange buttons…" from a device menu. Settings › Audio and video › Calls can also ask you before you leave a call.
- On Windows, Raven can show a notification when someone knocks on you. Turn it on in Settings › Notifications.

## Fixed

- Raven now lets go of your microphone when you leave a call, so Windows no longer shows it as in use while you're not talking to anyone.
