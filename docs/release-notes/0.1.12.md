# Raven 0.1.12

## Added

- **"I'd like to speak".** A new button in the bottom bar, shown during a call, lets the people in the room know you would like a turn, without interrupting whoever is talking. It does not ask anyone for permission: there is no host who grants the turn, and only you can raise or lower your own hand.
  - A hand appears beside your picture for everyone in the call. When more than one hand is up, each shows its place in line (1, 2, …). Raven never shows how long anyone has been waiting.
  - When you are first in line and nobody else has spoken for about a second and a half, your hand fills once and "Your turn" appears under your name. This is decided on your device; nothing extra is sent.
  - Your hand lowers itself after you have spoken for about three seconds with your microphone on. Speech while you are muted, or muted by someone else, does not count. You can still raise your hand while muted by someone else.
  - Stepping away with "Back in a bit", leaving the call or the call ending lowers it. The server keeps the time a hand went up only to put hands in order, never sends that time to anyone, and clears it when the hand comes down, so no record remains.
  - A keyboard shortcut, Ctrl+Shift+H by default (Cmd+Shift+H on macOS), raises and lowers your hand. It can be changed in Settings › Hotkeys.
  - You can hide the button in Settings › Appearance › Bottom bar. The shortcut keeps working when the button is hidden.
  - Others hear a short, soft note when someone raises a hand; it never plays when a hand comes down. It can be turned off in Settings › Audio and video › Sounds with "Someone would like to speak".
  - The voice overlay shows hands and their place in line. It can also show an "I'd like to speak" button, which is off until you turn it on in Settings › Voice overlay.

  Server operators: this needs the 0.1.12 API, which adds one column to call participants when it starts. Against an older server the button is not shown.

## Changed

- **A redesigned sidebar.** The sidebar now answers one question at a time. A switch at the top chooses between **Spaces** and **People** (your friends and groups). If you call them trees in Settings › Appearance, the switch says **Trees**. Raven remembers your choice on each device. The switch only changes when you change it: if you open something from the other side, such as a conversation from a notification, a "here" line under the switch names where you are, and clicking it shows that side.
- **Rooms under each space.** In the Spaces list, each space can be unfolded to show all its rooms, voice and text, in the order of its lobby. Live rooms show a dot and up to three of the people inside, and the room you are in says "Here". Clicking a voice room shows it before you join, as before; it never joins you directly. Spaces start unfolded, and Raven remembers which ones you fold on each device.
- **Easier to find Home, Settings and the Mixer.** The server name at the top of the sidebar has a house icon and goes to Home. A gear in the title bar opens Settings, which was only in the menu under your name. The Mixer button in the bottom bar shows its name on wider windows. Settings › Audio and video and the Mixer now link to each other.
- **One name for the bell.** The bell in the title bar is now called "Reaching you", the same as the list it opens and the section on Home. It was called "Inbox".
- **Home shows what is happening.** Home no longer repeats the list of spaces from the sidebar. It shows who is reaching you, live rooms, friends who are open to talk and unread conversations. When nothing is going on it says so. "Pick up" is now "Last visited".
- **Space lobbies.** A room that asks visitors to knock first, or is set to quiet, now says so in the lobby; open rooms show nothing extra. Your own row in the member list shows your role instead of "Set your name here"; setting a name for that space is still in its menu. The member list is headed "Around now", so it no longer looks like it contradicts the member count. On narrow windows the member list now appears below the rooms; before, it was hidden below 1100 pixels of width.
- **Set up your microphone before a call.** Noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain can now be changed outside a call, and apply when you join. Before, they were locked until your microphone had been used once. Raven now prepares the microphone's settings when it starts, but it does not open the microphone until you join a call or open the Mixer.
- **Notifications for knocks and messages are on by default.** Before, only event reminders were on. If you have changed any notification setting before, your saved settings are kept. The Notifications page also has clearer names for its sections: "New messages" for conversations and "Notes from Raven" for messages from Raven and your server.
- **Polls and room mentions no longer use the live colour.** The aqua colour now only marks something live: a live room, someone speaking or your own sound level. Poll answers use a check or a cross instead, and a room named in a message is no longer drawn in aqua.
- **Live calls at the top.** When a call is in progress, a Live section above both lists shows a card for each live room. Each card shows the room, its space and the people inside, with their pictures and names. The room you are in comes first and shows who is speaking, followed by rooms with your friends in them. Clicking a card shows the room before you join, and clicking a person opens their profile. You can fold the section to a single line that still counts the live rooms; Raven remembers this on each device. When nothing is live, the section is not shown.
- **Find someone.** The People list has a search field. It finds your friends and groups, people you share a space with, and archived conversations. The last result searches the whole server for the same text.
- **Lighter video.** Watching cameras and screen shares now takes much less of your computer's effort. Raven used to turn every frame into an image before showing it; it now hands frames straight to your graphics card to draw. In our tests with four videos open, Raven used about 40% less processor time and well under half the memory for drawing them, with no change in picture quality or frame rate. If your computer cannot draw video this way, Raven switches back to the previous method on its own.
- **Your own name in calls.** In a call, you now appear under your own name, or the name you use in that space, instead of "You". A small, muted "you" next to it shows which person is you; screen readers read it as "(you)". This applies to the room, camera tiles, picture in picture and the voice overlay.
- **Conversations follow new messages smoothly.** When you are at the end of a conversation, a new message, or someone starting to type, now slides into view over about a quarter of a second instead of jumping there. Several messages in a row move as one slide. If you have scrolled up to read, or gone to an older message, the view stays where you put it. When your system asks for reduced motion, or the window is hidden, the view moves without the slide.
- **A roomier GIF and sticker picker.** GIFs now fill each row edge to edge, each in its own shape, instead of one per row inside a fixed box with empty space around it. Stickers sit in an even grid. The picker is larger, and a new button beside the GIFs and Stickers tabs opens a larger view that covers the conversation above the message box, with bigger tiles and a list of your sections and collections on the left to jump between them. The picker opens at its normal size each time. Arrow keys move between rows as they appear on screen.
- **One list of friends and groups.** Offline friends stay in the list, dimmed and at the end, instead of in a separate section. Groups keep their square picture and now show how many people they have.
- **Mentions of people stand out.** A person mentioned in a message now sits in a softly tinted chip, so it reads as a mention and not as plain text. A mention of you is tinted more strongly.

## Fixed

- **Green or distorted video.** People sometimes saw a green, smeared tile where someone's camera or screen should be. This happened when the camera opened but sent no picture, or during the moment before the first picture arrived: the video library filled the gap with uninitialised frames. Raven now sends black until the first real picture. If a camera or share cannot start, it is turned off again and you are told why, instead of leaving a green tile for everyone else.
- **Virtual cameras such as OBS Virtual Camera.** Raven now opens cameras in the picture formats they offer (NV12, I420, YUY2 or RGB) instead of one fixed format. OBS Virtual Camera, which showed nothing before, now works. A camera that opens but sends no picture for four seconds is reported as such: "The camera opened but sent no picture. If it is a virtual camera, start it in the app that provides it."
- **Odd picture sizes.** Video whose width or height is an odd number of pixels is now drawn correctly instead of being rejected.
- **Screen shares on Linux.** On displays whose width is not a multiple of eight, such as 1366×768, shared screens were sheared and could go out of step. Raven now reads the rows as the capture pipeline lays them out.
- **Screen shares of another shape.** A screen or window whose shape differs from the chosen quality is now shown with black bars instead of being stretched. Cameras are unchanged.
- **A loading tile.** When you start watching a camera or a screen, the tile now says it is loading, for example "Loading Sam’s screen…", until the picture arrives. Before, it stayed blank for two or three seconds.
- **A failed camera no longer looks like a failed call.** When a camera or share fails, Raven reports that on its own; the call stays as it was and keeps reconnecting if the connection drops.
- **Steadier sound.** Small clock differences between a speaker's computer and yours are now corrected a little at a time, so incoming voices no longer drift into growing delay or restart their buffering every so often. Raven also no longer restarts a working screen share when only the viewing connection is lost, and leaving during a reconnect now leaves cleanly.
- **A mentioned person without a picture.** The mention showed an empty circle before the name. It now shows their first initial.

These were checked in test calls on Windows: OBS Virtual Camera reached the other side with real colours from the first frame, a shared screen showed its first frame about a second after it was watched, and an eight-second server outage recovered about five seconds after the server returned. The Linux screen-share change and the black-bar path are covered by tests only. macOS was not tested.

## Removed

- **The "All conversations" page.** Everything it listed is in the People list or can be found with Find someone, including archived conversations.
- **The list of people from your spaces and the separate offline list** in the sidebar. People you share a space with appear when you search for them.
