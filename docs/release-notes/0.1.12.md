# Raven 0.1.12

## Changed

- **A redesigned sidebar.** The sidebar now answers one question at a time. A switch at the top chooses between **Spaces** and **People** (your friends and groups). If you call them trees in Settings › Appearance, the switch says **Trees**. Raven remembers your choice on each device. It also switches for you when you open something from the other side, such as a conversation from a notification.
- **Live calls at the top.** When a call is in progress, a Live section above both lists shows a card for each live room. Each card shows the room, its space and the people inside, with their pictures and names. The room you are in comes first and shows who is speaking, followed by rooms with your friends in them. Clicking a card shows the room before you join, and clicking a person opens their profile. You can fold the section to a single line that still counts the live rooms; Raven remembers this on each device. When nothing is live, the section is not shown.
- **Find someone.** The People list has a search field. It finds your friends and groups, people you share a space with, and archived conversations. The last result searches the whole server for the same text.
- **Lighter video.** Watching cameras and screen shares now takes much less of your computer's effort. Raven used to turn every frame into an image before showing it; it now hands frames straight to your graphics card to draw. In our tests with four videos open, Raven used about 40% less processor time and well under half the memory for drawing them, with no change in picture quality or frame rate. If your computer cannot draw video this way, Raven switches back to the previous method on its own.
- **One list of friends and groups.** Offline friends stay in the list, dimmed and at the end, instead of in a separate section. Groups keep their square picture and now show how many people they have.

## Fixed

- **Green or distorted video.** People sometimes saw a green, smeared tile where someone's camera or screen should be. This happened when the camera opened but sent no picture, or during the moment before the first picture arrived: the video library filled the gap with uninitialised frames. Raven now sends black until the first real picture. If a camera or share cannot start, it is turned off again and you are told why, instead of leaving a green tile for everyone else.
- **Virtual cameras such as OBS Virtual Camera.** Raven now opens cameras in the picture formats they offer (NV12, I420, YUY2 or RGB) instead of one fixed format. OBS Virtual Camera, which showed nothing before, now works. A camera that opens but sends no picture for four seconds is reported as such: "The camera opened but sent no picture. If it is a virtual camera, start it in the app that provides it."
- **Odd picture sizes.** Video whose width or height is an odd number of pixels is now drawn correctly instead of being rejected.
- **Screen shares on Linux.** On displays whose width is not a multiple of eight, such as 1366×768, shared screens were sheared and could go out of step. Raven now reads the rows as the capture pipeline lays them out.
- **Screen shares of another shape.** A screen or window whose shape differs from the chosen quality is now shown with black bars instead of being stretched. Cameras are unchanged.
- **A loading tile.** When you start watching a camera or a screen, the tile now says it is loading, for example "Loading Sam’s screen…", until the picture arrives. Before, it stayed blank for two or three seconds.
- **A failed camera no longer looks like a failed call.** When a camera or share fails, Raven reports that on its own; the call stays as it was and keeps reconnecting if the connection drops.
- **Steadier sound.** Small clock differences between a speaker's computer and yours are now corrected a little at a time, so incoming voices no longer drift into growing delay or restart their buffering every so often. Raven also no longer restarts a working screen share when only the viewing connection is lost, and leaving during a reconnect now leaves cleanly.

These were checked in test calls on Windows: OBS Virtual Camera reached the other side with real colours from the first frame, a shared screen showed its first frame about a second after it was watched, and an eight-second server outage recovered about five seconds after the server returned. The Linux screen-share change and the black-bar path are covered by tests only. macOS was not tested.

## Removed

- **The "All conversations" page.** Everything it listed is in the People list or can be found with Find someone, including archived conversations.
- **The list of people from your spaces and the separate offline list** in the sidebar. People you share a space with appear when you search for them.
